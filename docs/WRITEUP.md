## 1. The Financial Hallucination Trap

Ask any modern Large Language Model to summarize an SEC 10-K or quarterly earnings deck, and it will generate fluent, authoritative prose. But ask it to calculate whether **Total Assets** actually equals **Total Liabilities + Stockholders' Equity**, or to evaluate whether a company's Operating Cash Flow supports its reported Net Income, and things quickly fall apart:

```
Reported Assets:       $1,245,000,000
Reported Liabilities:    $720,000,000
Reported Equity:         $480,000,000
Total Liabilities + Equity = $1,200,000,000  (Unbalanced by $45,000,000!)
```

LLMs are probabilistic token predictors, not algebraic solvers. Under pressure to "answer the prompt", models frequently commit the worst sin in quantitative finance: **hallucinating numbers to make the balance sheet balance**, or failing to notice when corporate presentations quietly truncate a chart's vertical axis to make a 4% uptick look like a 300% surge.

When we set out to build **FinSight-Gemma** for the Hacktoberfest Hack Day (organized by BITS Goa and SEDS Celestia), we established one inviolable architectural rule:

> **Gemma 4 reads the charts and tables. Deterministic Python computes and verifies the math. Never the other way around.**

## 2. Neuro-Symbolic Philosophy: Perception vs. Computation

A financial filing auditor requires two distinct capabilities:
1. **High-dimensional visual & semantic perception**: Navigating dense multi-column layouts, detecting footnote markers, parsing currency scales (millions vs. Indian Crores/Lakhs), and extracting data series from visual charts.
2. **Exact algebraic conservation**: Conservation laws do not tolerate a 0.1% tolerance margin. Assets must equal liabilities plus equity. Cash from operations minus capex must equal free cash flow. Discounted cash flows require exact compounding.

FinSight-Gemma bridges these two worlds using a neuro-symbolic feedback loop:

```
[Raw PDF / Image] 
       │
       ▼
 [ Gemma 4 VLM ] ──> [ Pydantic Schema ] ──> [ Accounting Solver ]
       ▲                                               │
       │                                               ▼
       └── [ Retry Prompt with Exact Residuals ] <── [ Violation? ]
                                                       │
                                                       ▼ [ Verified ]
                                             [ Forensic Reports ]
```

## 3. The Harness: Feedback Loops with Exact Residuals

Traditional RAG pipelines take model extraction outputs as final truth. FinSight-Gemma treats model outputs as hypotheses subject to mathematical verification.

When Gemma 4 extracts a balance sheet, our extraction harness immediately executes our symbolic accounting solver (`solver.py`). If the balance sheet identity fails, we do not throw a generic error. Instead, we calculate the exact residual and construct an algebraic correction prompt:

```python
# Generated by FinSight-Gemma Solver Loop
"Accounting check BALANCE_SHEET_IDENTITY failed: "
"total_assets (1,245.0) != total_liabilities (720.0) + total_equity (480.0). "
"Residual: +45.0. "
"Re-examine the balance sheet on page 42. Check for Non-Controlling Interests, "
"Additional Paid-in Capital, or scale unit confusion (thousands vs millions)."
```

Gemma 4 is given up to 3 retries with targeted residual feedback. If an identity remains broken after 3 attempts, FinSight-Gemma refuses to pretend the data is reliable: it flags the document as **UNVERIFIED (Exit Code 3)**, prints the residual, and preserves the evidence for human review.

## 4. Visual Forensics & Edward Tufte's Lie Factor

One of the most insidious practices in corporate earnings decks is the **truncated zero baseline**. By setting the $y$-axis minimum just below the lowest bar, a trivial increase appears monumental:

![Truncated Axis Illustration](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pln-shrm/finsight-gemma/main/demo/assets/lie_factor_concept.png)

To quantify chart dishonesty, we implemented statistician Edward Tufte's classic metric:

$$\text{Lie Factor} = \frac{\text{Size of Effect Shown in Graphic}}{\text{Size of Effect in Data}} = \frac{\frac{h_2 - h_1}{h_1}}{\frac{v_2 - v_1}{v_1}}$$

### How FinSight Audits Charts
1. **Semantic Extraction**: Gemma 4 extracts the chart title, printed data values ($v_1, v_2$), labeled axis limits, and detects whether a secondary axis is present.
2. **Computer Vision Measurement**: Using OpenCV morphology with vertical structuring elements (`cv2.morphologyEx`), we segment the bar geometry directly from the high-resolution page rendering and measure pixel heights ($h_1, h_2$).
3. **Deterministic Lie Factor**: If $LF < 0.95$ or $LF > 1.05$, the chart is flagged with the exact exaggeration ratio and page citation.
4. **Safety Classifiers**: If a chart has dual axes, it is classified as `UNSCORED` rather than generating false positives.

In our synthetic benchmark evaluation across varied baselines and noise distributions, FinSight achieved **100.0% Precision and 100.0% Recall** with an average audit latency of **39.3 ms**.

## 5. Non-GAAP Bloat & Forensic Ratios

Corporate reporting increasingly diverges from standard GAAP through non-GAAP adjustments ("Adjusted EBITDA", "Core Operating Income"). FinSight-Gemma automatically extracts non-GAAP reconciliation tables, performs footing checks ($GAAP + \sum adjustments == reported$), and computes the **Bloat Ratio**:

$$\text{Bloat Ratio} = \frac{\text{Reported Non-GAAP} - \text{GAAP Base}}{|\text{GAAP Base}|}$$

If bloat exceeds 25%, a forensic alert is triggered.

Additionally, FinSight executes four classical financial health models deterministically:
- **DuPont 5-Factor ROE Breakdown**: Separating operational margin, asset efficiency, leverage, and tax/interest burdens.
- **Altman Z''-Score**: Assessing bankruptcy risk without requiring equity market cap.
- **Beneish M-Score**: 8-variable probabilistic model for earnings manipulation risk.
- **Piotroski F-Score**: 9-point fundamental trend scorecard.

## 6. Distribution-First DCF: Replacing False Precision

Single-point stock price targets (e.g., "$142.50 per share") are financial fiction. Discounted Cash Flow models are intensely sensitive to small variations in Cost of Capital (WACC) and Perpetual Terminal Growth ($g$).

FinSight computes a **Distribution-First Valuation**:
- A $3 \times 3$ WACC vs. Growth Sensitivity Matrix.
- A 5,000-iteration Monte Carlo simulation perturbing revenue growth, operating margins, and discount rates to generate full $P_{10}$, $P_{50}$, and $P_{90}$ confidence intervals.

## 7. Open Weights on Laptop GPU vs. Cloud

Financial filings often contain sensitive, embargoed corporate data. Running analysis on third-party cloud servers can violate NDA and compliance rules.

FinSight-Gemma supports a dual-engine architecture:
- **Local (`--backend local`)**: Runs locally using Ollama and `gemma4:e4b` (4-bit quantized, consuming ~5.5 GB VRAM on our NVIDIA RTX 4050 laptop GPU). Zero document data leaves the machine.
- **Cloud (`--backend cloud`)**: Connects to Google DeepMind's `gemma-4-31b-it` via the Gemini API when parsing dense 10-column financial tables.
- **Cassette Replay (`--replay`)**: Stores cryptographic SHA-256 cassettes in `.cache/`, allowing auditors and judges to replay complex audits completely offline with zero network latency.
